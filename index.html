Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

What does the package include?

The listed offer is one mobile phone, three power banks and a five-month membership for ₦75,000. Review the current written terms before applying.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions must come from a verified official Telectro channel. The website does not process payments and does not ask for card PINs, bank passwords or one-time passcodes.

Why are bank details requested?

Bank account details are used only for eligible member payouts. They are private account information, visible to you and authorized administrators. Never provide a PIN, password, card data or OTP.

How do I check an application or payout?

Log in to your account. You can see your own application update and payout history there.
